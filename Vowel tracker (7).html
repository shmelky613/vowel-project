How the rounding percentage is worked out Take a saved unrounded vowel U and its rounded partner R (for example [i] and [y]). For a sound with F2 and F3:
A = (F2 - F2 of U) / (F2 of R - F2 of U)
B = (F3 - F3 of U) / (F3 of R - F3 of U)
Rounding = (A + B) / 2, shown as a percentage. 0% is your unrounded vowel and 100% is your rounded one. It works because rounding the lips lowers F2 and F3 together.
